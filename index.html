What is a Hipobuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Hipobuy spreadsheet means a personal worksheet for organizing product links you are researching for use with Hipobuy. It connects a source reference to the specific variant, measurements, evidence and questions you still need to check.

The download here is a blank planning CSV, not a verified product inventory or an official Hipobuy resource. It contains no preselected products, prices or purchase claims.

Start with the question behind the link

01 / Discover

Find a reference

Browse Fashionreps for product references, reviews and agent comparisons. Copy the actual product source into your sheet, alongside the page where you found it.

02 / Compare

Pin down the variant

Record color, size and measurements. Compare garment measurements with an item you own; do not treat the same letter size across sellers as equivalent.

03 / Decide

Resolve missing evidence

Keep unanswered questions visible. Review current seller and agent terms before paying, and obtain a current destination-specific shipping quote before planning a parcel.

How to use the CSV

  1. Download the blank file and open it in Excel, Google Sheets or another spreadsheet editor.
  2. Add one row per product and variant. Preserve the original source URL instead of only saving an image.
  3. Record the currency and date for each price; separate item cost, domestic shipping and the international shipping estimate.
  4. Link QC evidence to the actual order and variant when available. Leave unknown values blank and add a specific follow-up question.
  5. Recheck the source, current terms and parcel quote before deciding whether to proceed.

The CSV has headers only. This page does not save or upload your entries; your spreadsheet application handles the downloaded file. Avoid storing passwords, payment details or private delivery information in a shared sheet.

Before relying on a find

Confirm current fees, service availability, return conditions, storage rules and shipping restrictions directly with the relevant provider. This guide does not quote Hipobuy fees, discount codes, warehouse periods or delivery times because those require current official confirmation.

Check applicable import rules and intellectual-property restrictions for your destination. Do not infer that a product is genuine or lawful to import from its presence in a directory.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official Hipobuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent guide with a blank organizational template. It does not represent Hipobuy or process orders.

Where are the product links?

The promoted destination is fashion-reps.net. Research individual references there, then verify the original seller listing. The CSV intentionally contains no unverified product rows.

Does QC prove authenticity or long-term quality?

No. Photos can help you identify visible details and questions, but do not prove authenticity, durability, legal compliance or future performance.

Can the worksheet calculate a final landed cost?

No. It helps collect inputs. Ask for current shipping quotes and check exchange rates, provider fees, taxes and duties separately.
